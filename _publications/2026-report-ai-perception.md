description: "A July 2026 pilot survey of 500 graduating students and 500 workers in China, asking what they expect AI to do to their own jobs. One in three puts the five-year chance of being replaced at 50% or higher, yet fewer than one in ten expects the job to vanish. Workers who use AI daily expect a raise; graduating students expect to earn less and worry more. Two-thirds have already redirected their job search, moving in the direction their own pay expectations point. Almost everyone would take a free AI course and few would pay ¥800; those who drop out first are short of cash, not most afraid. Workers' replacement expectations track how much AI already does in their own job rather than their occupation's exposure score."
